# ADR 0010: MVP scope and quality baseline

## Context and proposed decision
Prefer image-focused managed assets, catalog permissions, metadata, search, previews and reliable deployment. Defer Cumulus migration, NAS, advanced workflow and versioning pending product owner confirmation. Proposed workload and quality gates in quality-mvp-roadmap.md. No release date or budget approved; #13 creates native implementation subissues after review.

## Consequences and acceptance
See the [consolidated architecture documents](../architecture/quality-mvp-roadmap.md) and the owning issue for test evidence, alternatives and residual risks. Review with affected issue owners; no approval or executed PoC is implied.
